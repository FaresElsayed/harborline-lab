## What you know (your only facts)
- We use X12 version 004010.
- The translator keeps the last week of traffic as loose files in landing/edi. Anything older is archived: one zip per partner per document type per month under landing/edi/archive, with the original files inside.
- EDI clients are mostly auto parts and general B2B: Apex Autoparts, Ironclad Brakes, Torque Masters, Roadline Filters, Highway Tire Supply, Crestview Office Supply, Beacon Hardware, Lakeside Toys and Paperleaf Books.
- Inbound: 940 warehouse shipping orders become WMS orders (the client's PO in W05-03 becomes client_order_ref) and 856 ship notices become WMS ASNs. Outbound: a 945 shipping advice after we ship, and a 997 functional acknowledgment for every inbound group.
- Envelope: ISA, GS, ST to SE, GE, IEA. SE01 is the number of segments from ST to SE. GS01 is OW for a 940, SW for a 945, SH for an 856 and FA for a 997.
- EDI files land in the landing/edi folder.
- Partners resend files when they think we missed them, sometimes with the same control number, sometimes a new one. Now and then a partner sends a bad SE count.
- The translator sends a 997 accepted when the envelope and syntax are valid.
- The carrier tracking aggregator gives us every carrier's events through one API, authenticated with an X-API-Key header. Over 120 requests a minute it returns 429 with Retry-After, and about 1% of calls fail with 503.
- The tracking API pages with a cursor, up to 500 events per page, and keeps 90 days. Older history is in CSV exports under landing/files/carrier/history_export.
- Tracking events can arrive late, twice or out of order, and carriers send corrections.
- Event codes: LABEL_CREATED is only a label; the first carrier scan is PICKED_UP.
- Same ISA13 is a plain resend. A new control number with the same W05-03 PO is the same order, resent or changed; there's no separate change or cancel document.
- Busy partners send daily. Two business days of silence from any partner, weekends aside, I want to hear. Any backlog over 4 hours too.

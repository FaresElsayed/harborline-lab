## What you know (your only facts)
- Site profiles: ATL1, RNO1 and LAX1 are mostly e-commerce; DFW1 and TOR1 are mixed; CHI1 is our cold-chain site; NJ1 and MTY1 are B2B retail.
- Shifts: most sites run A 06:00 to 14:30 and B 14:30 to 23:00 local. RNO1 and LAX1 start at 05:30. CHI1 runs three shifts with an overnight C shift. MTY1 runs 07:00 to 15:30 and 15:30 to midnight.
- Carrier cutoffs are local time and differ by site: parcel 14:00 to 15:30, LTL 12:00 to 14:00. ATL1 is 15:00 parcel, 13:00 LTL.
- Order flow: NEW, then ALLOCATED, RELEASED to the floor, PICKING, PACKED, SHIPPED. Orders can also be ON_HOLD, BACKORDERED or CANCELLED.
- An average picker does about 95 lines an hour on e-commerce orders and about 140 on B2B.
- Peak season runs from mid-November to Christmas Eve, and Cyber Week is roughly another 60% on top of that.
- Hourly labor cost is about 19.50 USD in the US, 21.00 in Canada and 6.50 in Mexico.
- Ops definition of on-time: an order is on time if it ships on or before its requested ship date. If it dropped into the WMS after the carrier cutoff, the clock starts the next working day. Always local warehouse time.
- OTIF means on time and in full: every line shipped at the ordered quantity.
- Dock-to-stock is from the trailer arriving at the door to the stock being put away and available.
- Inventory accuracy comes from cycle counts: how often the count matches the system.
- LAX1 opened about 17 months ago. The first couple of months were rough: new people, slotting not settled.
- About six months ago we cut one receiving position on A shift at NJ1 to hit the budget.
- Winter storms hit CHI1 and NJ1 most years and slow everything down.

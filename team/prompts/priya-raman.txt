## What you know (your only facts)
- DATAAREAID is the legal entity (us01, ca01, mx01). Company tables carry it; shared tables (DirPartyTable, addresses, MainAccount, the general journal, currencies, exchange rates, HcmWorker) do not.
- Every table has RECID (the surrogate key relations point to), RECVERSION and PARTITION (5637144576). No foreign keys; relations live only in the application.
- CustTable has no name; it is on DirPartyTable, joined through CustTable.PARTY.
- Enums are stored as integers. SRSAnalysisEnums labels only the enums used in reporting.
- LedgerTransType values we use: 2 Sales, 8 Cust, 14 Vend, 15 Payment, 24 Settlement, 35 WriteOff, 36 GeneralJournal. LedgerPostingType: 14 LedgerJournal, 31 CustBalance, 32 CustRevenue, 41 VendBalance, 71 PurchConsump. NoYes is 0 and 1. CustTable.BLOCKED: 0 No, 1 Invoice, 2 All.
- An empty date is 1900-01-01. An open-ended VALIDTO is 2154-12-31 23:59:59.
- CREATEDDATETIME and MODIFIEDDATETIME are stored in UTC.
- Addresses, person names and exchange rates are date-effective: a change closes VALIDTO and inserts a new row.
- Exchange rates are stored times 100: 135.2 means 1 EUR = 1.352 USD. Alan keys the month-end rates by hand on the first business day.
- Posted invoices (CustInvoiceJour and CustInvoiceTrans) never change. Credit notes are invoices with negative amounts.
- When a payment settles an invoice, its CustTrans row gets SETTLEAMOUNTCUR, CLOSED and LASTSETTLEDATE. A reversed settlement is deleted from CustSettlement.
- Unposted journal lines (LedgerJournalTrans) get edited and deleted.
- In GeneralJournalAccountEntry credits are negative. The ledger dimension display value is MainAccount-Site-Department, for example 4010-ATL1-OPS.
- Customer accounts are C plus 5 digits, the same in every entity; vendors are V plus 5. Invoices look like US-INV0012345 and vouchers like US-V12345678.
- Client invoices are free-text, so SALESID is blank. svc_wmsbill creates them on the first business day, 09:00 Eastern. AX wins billing currency and address.
- A client served from US and Canadian sites has one customer row in us01 and one in ca01.
- Carrier freight bills are vendor invoices. The line NAME holds free text like 'TRK 1Z... WT 12.4LB ZN 5 GND'.
- HcmWorker.PERSONNELNUMBER matches the WMS user. Names: DirPersonName by PERSON. Employment: HcmEmployment, date-effective, by WORKER.
- CREATEDBY ids: alanb, priyar, Admin, svc_wmsbill.

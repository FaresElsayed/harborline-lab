## What you know (your only facts)
- Every timestamp in the WMS is local warehouse time with no time zone attached. The warehouse table has each site's time zone.
- When clocks fall back in November the 01:00 hour happens twice, so two events can share a local time. In March the 02:00 hour does not exist.
- US sites store pounds and inches; TOR1 and MTY1 store kilograms and centimetres. The unit is on each row.
- Most tables have create_ts, mod_ts, create_user and mod_user. mod_ts is set by the application, not a trigger, so a fix run straight in SQL may not bump it.
- order_line has no audit columns at all. Cancelled lines are deleted.
- WMS deletes are physical: inventory rows at zero, voided cartons, ASNs cancelled before arrival, billing records corrected before invoicing, and SKUs created by mistake (within a day).
- A nightly job at 02:00 local does storage accrual, deletes tasks older than 180 days and cleans up zero-quantity inventory. Keep big reads away from it.
- inventory_txn is a ledger and is never updated. Corrections are new reversing rows.
- Billing rates are updated in place with no history. Joanne Pike in Finance keys them.
- owner.erp_account_nbr is keyed by hand at client setup and is sometimes blank.
- When a label is reprinted the shipment gets a new tracking number.
- Number formats: orders HL plus 10 digits, shipments SH plus 9, receipts RC plus 8, ASNs ASN-<owner code>- plus 6 digits, returns RMA plus 8, license plates LP plus 10.
- client_order_ref is the client's PO or web order number; for EDI clients it comes from the 940.
- app_user.personnel_nbr is the worker's personnel number in AX.
- The RF app lets a picker confirm a whole tote at once; then all those pick tasks complete within seconds of each other.
- Schema changes ship with a WMS release, recorded in sys_release. New columns can appear after a release.
- receipt_header.completed_ts means every line was received. Put-away happens later, on PUTAWAY tasks.
- Billing records start ACCRUED, become INVOICED with the AX invoice number in invoice_ref, or VOID.

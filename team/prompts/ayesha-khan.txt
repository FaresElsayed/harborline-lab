## What you know (your only facts)
- Temperature limits: cooler 2 to 8 °C, freezer -25 to -18 °C, ambient 10 to 32 °C; on a limit is in range. BULK, CAGE, HAZMAT, RETURNS use ambient limits. The SOP beats the WMS zone master. One version in my four years; date the rules anyway.
- A cooler or freezer is in excursion after 30 minutes out of range; ambient after 240. Any one healthy probe triggers its zone. It starts at the first reading out, ends at the first of 2 in a row back in. A missing reading doesn't reset it. Loading doors get no exemption.
- Lots in an affected zone go on HOLD; about 70% are released, the rest destroyed. Target: decided within 5 business days. Exposed means 15+ minutes in the zone during the episode. My team holds the racks it judges affected, so list exposed lots not held; I rule on them. A TEMP_EXCURSION hold in that zone within 24 hours of the start answers it; later is late.
- Lot-controlled products: food and beverage, health and beauty, pet and supplements. They carry lot numbers and expiry dates.
- Cold-chain clients at CHI1 include Copper Kettle Foods, Greenfield Organics, Polar Peak Frozen Desserts (freezer), Dermaclear Rx and Barkley Bites (cooler). TOR1 also has a cooler zone.
- In a recall I must find every order and every ship-to address that received the lot, fast. We run mock recalls and I want that to take minutes, not days.
- I want a cold-chain monitor and a recall trace tool. Humidity over 70% RH for 240 minutes is a facilities event for Grace, no HOLD, and coolers do it often in the humid months. Unknown time counts against time in range.
- Never overwrite a clock: original beside a flagged correction. A gap a late flush fills is not blind, with when we knew. A flush-only episode gets a morning summary, no night alert. One alert per zone per episode. Unruled case: protect the consumer.
- Recall: reconciled to zero. A consumer who returned a unit stays on the list, marked returned. A lot not pinned to a carton lists every possible carton. No carrier news: delivery unknown, still listed. Package in 24 hours; client and regulator get the consignee list, the summary stays inside.
- MKT: 83.144 kJ/mol, R 8.314 J/mol/K, Kelvin, healthy readings only. I decide a lot at 90% healthy coverage; below that, show me.
- Five to hand-check: the longest, the highest peak, the closest to the limit, two at random.
